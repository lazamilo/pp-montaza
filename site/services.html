Industrial installations

Installation of steel structures, pipelines, sanitary and electrical installations. We also dismantle, service and maintain industrial equipment and installations.

  • AlsoDismantling, service, maintenance

Orbital welding

Orbital welding is an automated arc welding process that joins two pipes in a butt joint. Every weld is repeatable and reliable.

Pipe welding requires constant control of the weld pool, its volume and its behaviour. The technology is used mostly for industrial pipelines, from energy to the food industry.

Because the process is automatic, orbital welds are 100% repeatable and reliable. We weld in TIG technology on pipes of different diameters and wall thicknesses.

  • ProcessTIG
  • PipesVarious diameters and wall thicknesses
  • Used inEnergy to food industry

Orbital cutting

A precise, square, burr-free cut and a perfectly bevelled pipe end. The basic requirement for productive, high quality automated welding.

Orbitalum pipe saws cut and bevel high alloy stainless steel, low alloy and unalloyed steel, plastics, cast materials and non-ferrous metals in seconds, using the planetary cutting method.

The clamping is powerful and leaves the workpiece without any deformation.

  • MethodPlanetary cutting
  • EquipmentOrbitalum pipe saws
  • MaterialsStainless, low and unalloyed steel, plastics, cast, non-ferrous

CNC pipe bending

With our CNC bending machine we bend all kinds of metal pipes, with walls up to 2 mm and diameters up to 42 mm.

CNC control keeps every bend identical, from a single prototype to a full series of pipe assemblies.

Bending in house means shorter routes, fewer welds and faster installation on site.

  • Wall thicknessup to 2 mm
  • Diameterup to 42 mm
  • MaterialsAll kinds of metal pipe
